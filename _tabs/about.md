## Hey there! 👋

I'm someone who's genuinely curious about cybersecurity and enjoys learning by doing. Most of my time is spent exploring web, API, and Android security, trying to understand how things work and how they can be broken (ethically, of course).

I also spend a lot of time solving CTF challenges because they're a fun way to learn new techniques and improve my problem-solving skills. Every machine or challenge teaches me something different, and that's what keeps me interested.

I'm always trying to learn something new—whether it's a new vulnerability, a tool I've never used before, or a technique that makes me think differently. There's always more to explore in cybersecurity, and that's one of the reasons I enjoy it so much.

When I'm not working or studying, you'll probably find me playing CTFs, reading security write-ups, or experimenting in my lab.
